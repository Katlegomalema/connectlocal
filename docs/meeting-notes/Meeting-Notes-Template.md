# Meeting Notes Template — ConnectLocal

## 1. Introduction
Standard template for recording team meetings, ensuring consistent, auditable documentation of decisions and action items.

## 2. Purpose
To create a reliable historical record of project decisions for both team coordination and academic assessment traceability.

## 3. Scope
Used for sprint planning, sprint review, retrospectives, and supervisor check-ins. Retrospective-specific content uses `docs/sprint-plans/Sprint-Retrospective-Template.md` instead.

## 5. Assumptions
- One team member is designated note-taker per meeting on a rotating basis.

## 6. Constraints
- Notes are committed to `docs/meeting-notes/` within 24 hours of the meeting to remain a reliable record.

## 7. Future Enhancements
- Link action items directly to GitHub Issues for automatic status tracking.
