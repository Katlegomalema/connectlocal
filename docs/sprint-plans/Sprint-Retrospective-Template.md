# Sprint Retrospective Template — ConnectLocal

## 1. Introduction
A standard template used at the end of every sprint to reflect on process, celebrate wins, and identify improvements.

## 2. Purpose
To drive continuous improvement in team process, communication, and delivery quality across the project lifecycle.

## 3. Scope
Applies to all sprints; completed retrospectives are saved as `Sprint-N-Retrospective.md` alongside the corresponding sprint plan.

## 5. Assumptions
- All team members contribute honestly and constructively to retrospective discussion (psychological safety is a team norm).

## 6. Constraints
- Retrospectives are timeboxed to 30 minutes to respect the team's limited availability alongside other coursework.

## 7. Future Enhancements
- Introduce anonymous input collection (e.g., a shared form) if open discussion proves less candid over time.
