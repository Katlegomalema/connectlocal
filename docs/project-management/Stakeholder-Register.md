# Stakeholder Register — ConnectLocal

## 1. Introduction
Identifies all individuals and groups with an interest in, or influence over, the ConnectLocal project.

## 2. Purpose
To ensure communication and engagement is appropriately targeted throughout the project lifecycle, and to clarify roles for decision-making and sign-off.

## 3. Scope
Covers internal (team, academic) and external (end-user proxy, service providers) stakeholders relevant to an academic capstone project.

## 4. Detailed Content

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Engagement Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project Lead / Backend Developer | Internal | Delivering a working system, architecture decisions | High | Daily involvement, sprint planning lead |
| Frontend Developer | Internal | Flutter app quality and UX delivery | High | Daily involvement, sprint execution |
| QA / Test Engineer | Internal | Test coverage and defect quality | Medium-High | Sprint reviews, test sign-off |
| UI/UX Designer | Internal | Wireframes, visual consistency | Medium | Design reviews prior to sprint start |
| Academic Supervisor | External (Academic) | Academic rigor, SDLC adherence, grading | High | Milestone reviews, fortnightly check-ins |
| External Project Assessor | External (Academic) | Final evaluation against rubric | High | Final demo and report review |
| Prospective Customers (proxy/testers) | External (End User) | Usable, trustworthy booking experience | Medium | Usability testing sessions, feedback surveys |
| Prospective Providers (proxy/testers) | External (End User) | Simple onboarding, fair commission, reliable bookings | Medium | Provider onboarding pilot, feedback sessions |
| Supabase / Render / Google / PayFast (platform vendors) | External (Technical) | Terms-of-service compliance, quota usage | Low-Medium | Monitored via documentation, no direct engagement needed |

## 5. Assumptions
- Proxy end-users (fellow students, friends/family in relevant trades) are available for usability testing given the absence of a live public user base.

## 6. Constraints
- No formal contractual relationship exists with third-party platform vendors beyond standard terms of service.

## 7. Future Enhancements
- Formal stakeholder engagement plan for a post-academic pilot with real paying customers and providers.
