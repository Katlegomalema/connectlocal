# Product Backlog — ConnectLocal

## 1. Introduction
The single, prioritized list of all user stories, technical tasks, and bugs pending implementation for ConnectLocal, maintained as the source of truth for sprint planning.

## 2. Purpose
To provide a living record of scope, allowing the team and supervisor to see what has been delivered, what is in progress, and what remains.

## 3. Scope
Reflects the current backlog state as of project kickoff; individual items are tracked live in GitHub Issues/Projects, with this document providing periodic snapshots for reporting.

### 4.2 Backlog Grooming Cadence
Reviewed and re-prioritized at the start of every sprint planning session; new items (bugs, technical debt) are triaged within 48 hours of being raised.

## 5. Assumptions
- Story point estimates use a Fibonacci scale (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) agreed by team consensus (planning poker) during backlog refinement.

## 6. Constraints
- Backlog is capped to what fits the ~11-sprint academic timeline; excess items are deferred to Future Enhancements.
